Privacy

This website doesn't track you, and anything you share during a build stays yours.

Who I am

KEVIN is built and sold by me, Anthony Maisano. Send privacy questions to amaisao48@gmail.com.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ads or trackers. It has no forms. Its fonts, scripts and videos load from the site itself.

GitHub Pages hosts the site, and GitHub keeps visitors' IP addresses in its logs for security. GitHub's privacy statement explains how it handles them.

When you email me

I get your email address and whatever you put in the message. I use it to answer you and to talk about a build. I don't sell it or share it for marketing.

During a build

Your files, logins and data stay yours, on your own computers and accounts. I work inside your setup only with your permission, and only on the work we agreed. You decide what I can reach, and you can take that access back at any time. I delete my working copy at handover.

AI providers

KEVIN runs on AI accounts you own and pay for, with Anthropic, OpenAI or any other provider you plug in. What you send them follows their rules and your account settings. I never use your data to train a model.

Keeping and deleting

I keep emails and project records only as long as I need them to answer you, do the work and keep normal business records. Ask me to delete what I hold about you and I will, unless the law says I have to keep it.

Your requests

Email me to see what I hold about you, to correct it or to delete it.

Changes

When this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
