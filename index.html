    Every agent, one screen.

    A tab for each agent, each on its own model. A lead agent splits the work, a second AI checks it, and you approve from one place.

  2. // 02 Models

    Claude and GPT. One brain.

    Opus, Sonnet, Haiku, Fable and GPT side by side. New models get added to the picker on the care plan.

  3. // 03 Brain

    Persistent memory, built for AI to read.

    Your rules, prices and lessons live in plain files you own, laid out so the AI reads them the same way every session. Correct it once and the lesson gets saved, then applied to the skill it belongs to.

  4. // 04 Skills and robots

    The repeat work, on a timer.

    Quotes, follow-ups, invoice reminders and ad reports. Robots run the backup overnight and have your morning brief ready when you wake up.

  5. // 05 Hooks

    Nothing reaches a customer without your yes.

    Code that runs before the AI acts. Customer messages are held. The money and delete tools you name are blocked.

Open the folder.

Everything KEVIN knows lives in plain files you can read. These are the real formats, from a sample build.

The operating manual. Every agent reads this first, on any model. Laws, startup steps and voice live here. Codex reads the same rules from AGENTS.md.

The memory. Each correction becomes a dated line. Agents read the newest lessons at the start of every session.

A skill. One repeat job, written as steps the AI follows. Skills get rewritten when a lesson shows a better way.

A hook. This tells KEVIN to run a check before the AI sends, refunds or deletes anything.

The lock itself. Plain code. When it exits with code 2, the action stops and the AI gets told why.

# HALVORSEN PLUMBING: operating system
You run the office for Halvorsen Plumbing. Read this first, every session.

## On startup
1. Read brain/00_STATUS.md, then the brain file for today's job.
2. Read the newest 10 lines of brain/LESSONS.md.

## Laws
1. Nothing reaches a customer without Mike's yes.
2. Prices come from brain/PRICES.md. Never invent one.
3. Every lesson goes in brain/LESSONS.md the day it happens.

## Voice
Plain English. Short sentences. Sign texts "Mike, Halvorsen Plumbing".

  Added to quote-from-notes.

  until the 16th.

---
name: quote-from-notes
description: Turn a job's notes and photos into a priced
  quote draft. Use when Mike says "quote" plus a job name.
---
1. Read jobs/<job>/notes.md and any photos.
2. Price every line from brain/PRICES.md. Flag anything
   with no price.
3. Add permit and haul-away when the job replaces equipment.
4. Save the draft to drafts/quotes/. Never send it.
5. Show Mike the total and the lines he should check.

  }

"""Runs before the AI acts. Exit code 2 stops the action."""
import json, sys
from approvals import approved

call = json.load(sys.stdin)
tool = call["tool_name"]

if tool in ("refund", "delete_file"):
    print("Money moves and deletions are locked.", file=sys.stderr)
    sys.exit(2)

if tool.startswith("send_") and not approved(call):
    print("Held: customer messages wait for Mike's yes.", file=sys.stderr)
    sys.exit(2)

Questions.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You ask in plain English. The walkthrough covers every button.

Which AI models does it run?

Claude (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku and Fable) and GPT through Codex today. On the care plan, I add supported new models to the picker after I test them.

Will it send anything on its own?

No. It drafts and waits. You approve each message before it goes out.

Who sees my data?

Your files live on your computer and in your private backup. What you ask the AI goes to the model provider you choose (Anthropic or OpenAI) under your own account. I work inside your setup during the build and delete my working copy at handover.

Can it talk?

Mine answers to "Hey Kevin." Voice is a separate add-on once yours has run for 30 days.

What if something breaks?

Fixes are free for 30 or 60 days, depending on your build. After that, the care plan covers it.
